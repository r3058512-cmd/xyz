FAQ

Quick answers

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no daily limit. Open a tool and use it.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

Not by default. Images, PDFs and text are processed inside your browser and stay on your device. The only exception is the optional AI mode in the Image Quality Increaser and Image Background Remover. When it is ticked, that one image is sent through this site to the AI service and is not kept by this site.

Which image formats can I use?

You can add JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF and BMP files. The tools save as JPG, PNG or WebP. Some browsers can also open AVIF files.

What happens to a transparent PNG?

JPG has no transparency, so transparent areas turn white. Choose WebP or PNG if you need to keep the see-through background.

How well do Word to PDF and PDF to Word work?

Word to PDF keeps headings, bold and italic text, lists, tables and pictures. Fonts are swapped for standard ones, and headers, footers and text boxes are left out. Files that contain Hindi or other non-English scripts are saved as picture pages, so their text cannot be selected. PDF to Word needs a PDF with selectable text. It rebuilds paragraphs, headings and bullet points, but tables become plain lines, pictures are not copied, Hindi and other non-English text often comes out wrong, and scanned PDFs cannot be read.

How accurate are the converters?

Length, weight, area, volume, temperature, speed, time and data converters use standard exact values and work without internet. The currency converter needs internet and uses exchange rates that update once a day, so treat the answer as a guide. Your bank or card may charge a different rate and fees.

Can I convert a PDF to JPG?

Not yet. Today you can turn images or Word files into a PDF, turn a PDF into Word, and merge several PDFs into one.

How good are the background remover and quality increaser?

Without AI mode both run in your browser. The remover works best on plain, evenly lit backgrounds such as ID photos and signatures. The quality increaser enlarges and sharpens, but it cannot add detail that is missing from the original photo. AI mode, when available, usually gives cleaner results.
